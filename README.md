 Overview
This report provides a comprehensive analysis of retail store performance, customer behavior, and product profitability using Power BI dashboards. The analysis spans transactional data across multiple regions, customer segments, and timeframes, offering key insights for strategic decision-making.

2. Financial Performance

Year-to-Date Revenue: $1.76 million

Monthly Profit Decline: A 4.18% drop in the most recent month suggests the possibility of seasonal or promotional impact.

3. Store-Level Insights
Store 13 and Store 17 emerged as the highest contributors to company profit, driven by both high transaction volume and exceptional weekend performance.

Weekend Sales: Contribute approximately 30% of total transactions and are crucial to store profitability, especially for top-performing outlets.

Top Performing Brand: Hermanos, with over $21.5K in profit and a 58.6% profit margin.

Most Returned Brand: Also Hermanos, highlighting a potential quality or perception issue worth investigating.

Key Product Driver: Hermanos Green Pepper ranks highest in profitability, contributing significantly to overall sales success.

5. Customer Segmentation and Demographics

Ideal Customer Profile: Married homeowners with 1–2 children—exhibit high spend, high loyalty, and low return rates.

6. Regional Trends
Top Markets: USA, Mexico, and Canada

High-Growth Regions: Central and North West Mexico

Underperforming Regions: DF, Yucatan, Veracruz, Guerrero, and Jalisco show lower weekend profitability and require localized strategies.

7. Temporal Trends
Revenue Exceeds Target: In 1998, revenue surpassed targets by over $10K, reflecting effective business operations.

YTD Revenue Growth: Demonstrates a positive trend over the previous year (1997), with strong performance across most regions.

Weekend-Specific Products: Items like Tri-State Onions and CDR Vegetables performed disproportionately well during weekends, highlighting promotional opportunities.

8. Strategic Recommendations
Investigate MoM Profit Decline: Examine regional or campaign-related factors that may be affecting monthly sales.

Improve Product Quality Control: Focus on brands with high return rates to enhance customer satisfaction and reduce losses.

Leverage Loyal Customer Segments: Target campaigns toward profitable demographics such as gold members and families.

Maximize Weekend Opportunities: Tailor promotions around weekend behavior to boost high-traffic periods.

Revitalize Underperforming Regions: Implement localized marketing and inventory strategies in low-revenue areas.
